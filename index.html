Our Disciplines

  • Wushu

    Chinese martial arts forms and Sanda, the sport’s sparring discipline.

  • Wing Chun

    A traditional Chinese system for self-defence that emphasises structure and close range technique.

  • MMA

    Mixed martial arts (MMA) combines striking, takedowns and grappling.

  • Boxing

    Punching, defence, timing and footwork.

  • Muay Thai

    Thai striking with punches, kicks, knees, elbows and clinch work.

  • Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu

    Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) focuses on position, escapes and submissions.

Our Legacy

In 1975, Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera introduced Wushu to Sri Lanka. He founded the Sri Lanka National Wushu Academy and helped bring Wushu into school sport.
