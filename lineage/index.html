Shanghai to Sri Lanka

Chin Woo Wushu

The original Chin Woo family tree traces the tradition from its Shanghai roots to the teachers who established it in Sri Lanka.

Key moments

  1. 1910

    The Shanghai roots

    Huo Yuanjia and Nong Jinsun establish the training centre that grows into the Chin Woo Athletic Association.

  2. 1975

    Wushu in Sri Lanka

    Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera introduces Wushu to Sri Lanka.

  3. 1989

    Sifu Sun visits Sri Lanka

    At Sifu Prasanna’s invitation, Sifu Sun Jin Quan visits from Shanghai. They spend nearly two years promoting Chin Woo Wushu together.

  4. The years that followed

    A Sri Lankan association

    Their collaboration leads to the establishment of the association. It later sends athletes to Shanghai for advanced study.
