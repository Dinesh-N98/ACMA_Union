Global Traditional Wing Chun Kung Fu Association – Sri Lanka

Traditional Wing Chun in Sri Lanka, taught through the lineage of Ip Man, William Cheung and Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera and affiliated with the Global Traditional Wing Chun Kung Fu Federation.

History and lineage

The Traditional Wing Chun lineage

Grandmaster William Cheung studied under Ip Man, whose name is also written Yip Man. Cheung established the Global Traditional Wing Chun Kung Fu Federation to preserve and teach the system he learned. Master Andrew Cheung, William Cheung’s son, is listed as the federation’s president.

The lineage in Sri Lanka

Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera learned Traditional Wing Chun from William Cheung and carried that teaching into his work in Sri Lanka. His earlier academy account lists Cheung among the instructors who shaped his training. The federation’s register identifies Prasanna Jayaweera as Sri Lanka branch chief and Sifu Sajith Jayaweera as deputy branch chief.

Continuing the teaching tradition

Sajith Jayaweera continues the work of his father and teacher, Prasanna Jayaweera. In 2025, students and international guests including Andrew Cheung attended a tribute in Colombo marking 50 years of Prasanna Jayaweera’s work in Sri Lankan Wushu.
