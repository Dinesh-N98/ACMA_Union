Chin Woo Wushu Association – Sri Lanka

The Chin Woo Wushu Association in Sri Lanka, with ties to the Shanghai Chin Woo Athletic Association and the Wushu teaching legacy of Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera.

History and lineage

Chin Woo’s roots in Shanghai

In 1910, Huo Yuanjia and Nong Jinsun established a physical training centre in Shanghai that became the forerunner of the Chin Woo Athletic Association. Chin Woo, also written Jingwu, grew into an international movement for Chinese martial arts and physical education. Shanghai marked the association’s 115th anniversary in 2025.

Wushu in Sri Lanka

Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera introduced Wushu to Sri Lanka in 1975 and founded the Sri Lanka National Wushu Academy, historically based in Jayanthipura, Battaramulla. In his academy history, he recalls training in China and learning from instructors including Sun Jianqun, Guan Tieyun and William Cheung. ACMA Union’s archive includes a 1987 portrait with Master Sun, whose name is also rendered Sun Jian Qwan in the photo caption.

How Chin Woo took root in Sri Lanka

In 1989, Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera invited Shanghai Chin Woo chief instructor Sifu Sun Jin Quan to Sri Lanka. Their mutual friend, Dr Chang Long Wang of the Chinese Medical Centre, put them in touch. Sun spent nearly two years in Sri Lanka promoting Chin Woo Wushu alongside Jayaweera. The association’s original history credits their work together with the establishment of the Sri Lanka Chin Woo Wushu Association.

The same account records more than a thousand graduates by the time it was written. Sri Lankan athletes also travelled to Shanghai for advanced study, with support from Sifu Sun. The association’s archive records permanent membership in the Shanghai Chin Woo Athletic Association and membership in the International Chin Woo Athletic Federation.

From the academy to school sport

Jayaweera’s academy history records national recognition for Wushu in 1987 and its acceptance as a school sport in 1990. The same account describes the formation of the Sri Lanka Wushu Federation in 1993. Royal College records that its Wushu programme began in 1992, with Jayaweera as its first coach.

International exchange

The academy’s account records appearances by its Sri Lankan athletes in Japan in 1988, Hong Kong in 1989, South Korea in 1990, Malaysia in 1991, the Philippines in 1992, Macau in 1993 and Chinese Taipei in 1994. The international Chin Woo directory maintained by the Malaysian association also lists the Sri Lanka Chin Woo Athletic Association.
