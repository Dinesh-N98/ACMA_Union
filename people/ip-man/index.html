Ip Man

Wing Chun teacher, also known as Yip Man, and teacher of William Cheung.

Ip Man taught Wing Chun to William Cheung. In the account published by the Global Traditional Wing Chun Kung Fu Federation, Cheung describes the federation’s mission as preserving the Traditional Wing Chun he learned from Ip Man.
