Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera

Founder of the Sri Lanka National Wushu Academy and Sri Lanka branch chief in the Global Traditional Wing Chun Kung Fu Federation.

In 1975, Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera introduced Wushu to Sri Lanka. He established the Sri Lanka National Wushu Academy and helped develop Wushu as a school sport. Royal College records that he became its first Wushu coach in 1992.

Jayaweera’s academy history names Sun Jianqun and William Cheung among his teachers. Through his training with Cheung, he carried Traditional Wing Chun teaching to Sri Lanka. The international federation lists him as Sri Lanka branch chief.

A tribute in Colombo in 2025 marked 50 years of his contribution to Wushu and brought together students, instructors and international guests.

In 1989 Jayaweera invited Sifu Sun Jin Quan to Sri Lanka. Their collaboration led to the establishment of the Sri Lanka Chin Woo Wushu Association, according to the association’s original history.
