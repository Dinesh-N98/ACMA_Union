Master Andrew Cheung

President of the Global Traditional Wing Chun Kung Fu Federation.

The federation identifies Andrew Cheung as its president, a Traditional Wing Chun instructor, and the son of Grandmaster William Cheung. He attended the 2025 Colombo tribute marking Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera’s 50 years of work in Wushu.
