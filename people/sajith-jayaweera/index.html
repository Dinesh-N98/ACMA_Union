Sifu Sajith Jayaweera

Deputy branch chief for Sri Lanka in the Global Traditional Wing Chun Kung Fu Federation.

Sajith Jayaweera continues the work of his father and teacher, Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera. The international federation lists Sajith as deputy branch chief for Sri Lanka.

The Chin Woo family tree also records Sajith’s training in Chin Woo Wushu under his father, Sifu Prasanna Jayaweera.
